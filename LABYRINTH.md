# The Labyrinth: lore by descent

The Labyrinth is one spiral of 40 floors, split into 8 descents of 5 floors. Each descent ends at a sealed gate held by a Shadow guardian. Each guardian was a protector of its layer before the Great Calamity. Zoroark takes their Shadow power each time one falls.

Add your own lines under **Your notes** for each descent.

## Descent 1 · The Lantern Stair (floors 1–5)
- **Background:** `labyrinth-1`
- **Leads from → to:** Lumen Rest → Verdant Hollow
- **What you see:** carved stone stairs lit by old iron lanterns. Purple crystals in the walls. Tree roots breaking through the ceiling. A long green-lit tunnel and a rope-railed path leading down into the dark.
- **Lore:**
  - The first explorers carved these stairs 500 years ago, when Pokémon first fled into the Labyrinth.
  - The lanterns are lit by the Explorers' Guild. Each graduating class lights one more before they go down.
  - The roots belong to Verdant Hollow's great trees. They grew up toward Lumen Rest, looking for light.
  - The green glow below is the first spore-light of the Hollow. Since the Calamity, it flickers like it's sick.
- **Wild Pokémon feel:** young, curious and easily startled. Grass, Bug and Normal types.
- **Gatekeeper:** Shadow Scyther, *the Pale Blade*. It once guarded the forest. Nurse Mabel knew it.
- **Story hook:** the team's own class lantern. Zoroark is first seen here as a shadow passing the lanterns.

## Descent 2 · The Frostroot Falls (floors 6–10)
- **Background:** `labyrinth-2`
- **Leads from → to:** Verdant Hollow → Tidecaller Grotto
- **What you see:** giant ancient roots coiled over black rock, hung with icicles. Frozen waterfalls glowing pale blue. Ice crystals growing out of the stone. Mist rising from a cracked, wet stone floor.
- **Lore:**
  - These are the deepest roots of the Hollow's oldest tree, the one the forest is named for. They reach all the way down to the underground sea.
  - The Calamity froze the falls mid-pour. The ice glows because the water is still trying to move under it.
  - Explorers say the roots creak when someone is about to fall. Most of them listen.
- **Wild Pokémon feel:** Grass meets Ice and Water. Cold, slow and patient.
- **Gatekeeper:** Shadow Lapras. Old Maren's child, lost in the Calamity.
- **Story hook:** the team hears singing under the ice. It's the same song Maren sings to the boats.

## Descent 3 · The Thousand Falls (floors 11–15)
- **Background:** `labyrinth-3`
- **Leads from → to:** Tidecaller Grotto → Stormspire Highlands
- **What you see:** tall black cliffs with waterfalls pouring from every ledge. Glowing blue pools. Natural stone arches. Storm clouds and lightning crackling across the cave ceiling far above.
- **Lore:**
  - The underground sea spills upward and outward here, falling through a hundred stone terraces.
  - The ceiling holds its own storm: clouds born from the endless spray. Lightning from Stormspire leaks down through cracks.
  - The stone arches are what's left of an old bridge city. No one knows who built it.
- **Wild Pokémon feel:** Water and Electric. Fast, loud and territorial.
- **Gatekeeper:** Shadow Zapdos. It used to keep the storm in balance; now the storm keeps it.
- **Story hook:** a rival (Zephyr) tries to fly across the falls and gets caught in the lightning.

## Descent 4 · The Divide (floors 16–20)
- **Background:** `labyrinth-4`
- **Leads from → to:** Stormspire Highlands → Emberdeep Caldera
- **What you see:** a vast cavern split in two. On the left, cold purple cliffs, storm mist and a thin waterfall. On the right, the rock glows red-hot with rivers of lava. A narrow switchback path winds between the two.
- **Lore:**
  - This is where the storm and the fire meet and never mix. The path down follows the exact line between them.
  - Step too far left and the wind takes you. Step too far right and the ground burns.
  - Explorers leave a stone on the path for each friend they lost here. The piles are taller since the Calamity.
- **Wild Pokémon feel:** split by side: Flying and Electric on the cold side, Fire and Ground on the hot side.
- **Gatekeeper:** Shadow Moltres. It once kept the Caldera's fires low enough to live beside.
- **Story hook:** the team has to choose which side to cross on. The choice changes the encounter.

## Descent 5 · The Lantern Drift (floors 21–25)
- **Background:** `labyrinth-5`
- **Leads from → to:** Emberdeep Caldera → Duskwood Necropolis
- **What you see:** lava cooling into black rock on one side. The other side fades into purple twilight. Paper lanterns float in the air with no one holding them. A long stone bridge stretches into violet mist.
- **Lore:**
  - The fire dies here, and the Necropolis begins.
  - The floating lanterns drift up from Duskwood. Each one is said to carry the memory of someone lost in the Calamity.
  - If a lantern follows you, someone you lost is watching. If it flies away, they've let you go.
- **Wild Pokémon feel:** Fire fading into Ghost and Dark. Quiet, curious, a little sad.
- **Gatekeeper:** Shadow Gengar. It used to guide lost souls; now it collects them.
- **Story hook:** a lantern follows Litwick. Sister Wick will want to know why.

## Descent 6 · The Crystal Crypts (floors 26–30)
- **Background:** `labyrinth-6`
- **Leads from → to:** Duskwood Necropolis → Mirage Sanctum
- **What you see:** an old crypt hall with stone coffins, urns and candles in alcoves, and banners on the walls. The right wall is overgrown with huge purple crystals. One crystal shows a reflection of a place that isn't there: a lit balcony and a lamp.
- **Lore:**
  - The Necropolis buried its oldest dead here, long before the Calamity.
  - The crystals of Mirage Sanctum are growing down into the crypts. They show what the dead remember.
  - Never stare into a crystal for too long. Some explorers walked into a reflection and didn't come back.
- **Wild Pokémon feel:** Ghost into Psychic and Fairy. Things that show you what you want to see.
- **Gatekeeper:** Shadow Gardevoir. It protected the Sanctum's dreamers; now it traps them in their own dreams.
- **Story hook:** a crystal shows Riolu a Lucario it once failed. It's the first glimpse of the Mirror of Regrets.

## Descent 7 · The Rusted Vein (floors 31–35)
- **Background:** `labyrinth-7`
- **Leads from → to:** Mirage Sanctum → Ironroot Foundry
- **What you see:** glowing purple crystal walls on the left. On the right, huge rusted pipes, tanks and iron frames, with bright green toxic water dripping into channels. Iron bridges arch overhead into the distance.
- **Lore:**
  - The dead machine city of Ironroot dug its pipes up into the crystal to feed on its glow. The crystals are growing back over the pipes.
  - The green runoff comes from engines that never stopped running. It's slowly poisoning the crystal from below.
  - Some say the machines are learning to dream from the crystals. That's why they're getting louder.
- **Wild Pokémon feel:** Psychic and Fairy giving way to Steel and Poison. Strange mixes of both.
- **Gatekeeper:** Shadow Metagross. It once ran the city's machines with care; now it runs them without stopping.
- **Story hook:** Forgemother Ferra's forge marks are on the oldest pipes. They're the same marks as on Aron's shell.

## Descent 8 · The Spine of the World (floors 36–40)
- **Background:** `labyrinth-8`
- **Leads from → to:** Ironroot Foundry → Primordial Throne
- **What you see:** an iron walkway with green-lit lamps and pipes on the left. On the right, gigantic bones: a spine and curved ribs, each as tall as a tower, embedded in the rock. Everything glows orange from the heat below.
- **Lore:**
  - The machine city was built on top of a titan's skeleton. Its pipes run between the ribs.
  - The bones are warm. Explorers swear they can feel a slow heartbeat through the floor.
  - At the bottom lies the Primordial Throne, and somewhere there, the First Egg.
- **Wild Pokémon feel:** Steel and Dragon. Ancient, powerful and rarely seen.
- **Gatekeeper:** Shadow Lugia, the last and strongest guardian. It sealed the First Egg once. The Calamity broke that seal.
- **Who's already here:** Guildmaster Basalt, a Tyranitar, missing for 30 years. He went to the source of the Calamity alone and has been holding Shadow Lugia back at this gate ever since. The sandstorm that grows thicker on every floor is his. Each floor deeper, the team finds signs of him: claw marks in the bones, a cracked boulder wall, a sandstorm that seems to point the way.
- **Story hook:** this is where Zoroark is weakest and most desperate. The team may find her before the bottom.
