# Story

## Background

- **The war (500 years ago).** Pokémon fought a war with the humans and fled into the Labyrinth for shelter. The Labyrinth gave many of them intelligence and a voice. Some stayed wild.
- **500 years of peace.** Towns grew on every layer. Almost nobody learned to battle.
- **The Great Calamity (30 years ago).** Shadow Pokémon appeared and sealed the gate on every layer, splitting families apart. The Shadow Pokémon were locked away in the dungeons. The Labyrinth turned deadly, and wild Pokémon in the overworld turned aggressive. Explorers try not to bother the wild ones, but sometimes they can't avoid a fight.
- **Now.** The Explorers' Guild trains young Pokémon to fix what the Calamity broke. Our heroes are this year's graduates.

## Villages

Every village is led by a guildmaster who posts quests. Each is a different kind of Pokémon with a different way of leading:

- **Orrin** (Noctowl), Lumen Rest: wise, dry humour, carries the weight of every class he sends down.
- **Ivy** (Meganium), Verdant Hollow: gentle and fierce. Orrin's mate.
- **Maren** (Lapras), Coralight Harbor: old and patient. Sings to the boats.
- **Volta** (Luxray), Voltcrest Outpost: stern, fair, always short on scouts.
- **Smolder** (Torkoal), Cinder Sanctum: slow-talking monk, quietly afraid.
- **Wick** (Chandelure), Lanternwake Chapel: keeper of lost souls' lanterns.
- **The Watcher** (Xatu), Mindspire Observatory: sees too much, says too little.
- **Ferra** (Aggron), Forgeheart Bastion: hammers steel day and night.
- **Basalt** (Tyranitar), Primordial Throne: **missing for 30 years.** The strongest guildmaster who ever lived. Intense, blunt, impossible to impress. He walked alone to the source of the Calamity and was never seen again. Elder Vael (Dragonite) leads in his place and still sets a place for him at every meal.

## Families
Pokémon pair up with whoever they love, not with their own species. Families are mixed and that's part of the fun:

- **Orrin & Ivy** (Noctowl & Meganium) met as young explorers on a rescue mission and still argue about who saved whom. Their children are **Sage** (a Hoothoot who can't sit still) and **Clover** (a Chikorita who hasn't forgiven her father for not coming sooner).

## The villain

Zoroark ("The Trickster") disguises herself as other Pokémon. Each gate guardian holds immense Shadow power. She takes that power from every guardian the team defeats, hoping to reach the source of the Calamity, the First Egg, even if it means starting it again.

**Motive:** she is searching for her older sister, who was lost in the Calamity. She believes the Egg can bring her back. The power makes her stronger but is killing her, and she dies before she reaches it.

## The last gate: Basalt and Shadow Lugia

Thirty years ago, Guildmaster Basalt went alone to the First Egg to stop the Calamity at its source. He couldn't destroy it, so he never left. He stands at the last gate holding back **Shadow Lugia** with his own body, and has for 30 years. The sandstorms on the Labyrinth's deepest floors are his.

When the team reaches floor 40, Basalt is there: scarred, exhausted, still standing. He doesn't thank them. He tells them they're late. Then he fights beside them.

## The First Egg

A Red Rift on the deepest layer (Primordial Throne): a chamber older than the Labyrinth itself. A cracked egg of pure Shadow sits at its heart. When it cracked open 30 years ago, that was the Great Calamity. Fed enough Shadow power, it will hatch again. This is what Zoroark is searching for. Elder Vael saw it happen.

## Chapter 1: Graduation

1. **Graduation day.** Orrin calls each graduate up by name. The team picks its name and gets its explorer badges, plus **2 Escape Ropes** for the Labyrinth. *(DM: press "Graduation day" on the World tab's Quests card.)* Team Razorwind graduates at the top of the class and lets everyone know it.
2. **The graduation spar.** The tradition is a friendly match between the top two teams: the players against Razorwind, at the same levels. The winner gets first pick from Orrin's reward chest. Each rival targets the player it chose to beat. *(Set it up in the Battle tab.)*
3. **First-aid lesson.** Nurse Mabel patches everyone up after the spar and scolds both teams. Pebble asks if bandages are edible. Mark Mabel as met and press + once, so the team starts as Friendly and field heals are half price.
4. **Graduation sale.** Lark and Lev hold a sale for the new graduates. This is the first chance to haggle, so let every player try a Charisma roll for practice.
5. **The quest board.** Orrin posts three starter jobs (already on the Quests card after graduation): rescue a lost Pichu, gather Oran Berries for Lei, and drive off a wild Pokémon that keeps raiding the town's supply carts.
6. **Something's off.** On the way back, "Orrin" stops the team and sends them the wrong way, toward a pack of wild Pokémon. The tell: Noctowl's eyes glow red, not gold, and he hoots in daylight. That was Zoroark's first appearance. Don't reveal it yet.
7. **Race to the first gate.** Razorwind rushes into the Labyrinth first. Partway down, the team finds Zephyr hurt and Razorwind pinned down. Saving them, or leaving them, sets the rivalry for the rest of the game.
8. **The gate opens.** The team beats the Gatekeeper. Orrin arrives. The gate grinds open and everyone flinches, but Orrin walks forward and smiles at the team. On the other side is Guildmaster Ivy, a Meganium, with Sage and Clover beside her. Sage crashes into Orrin's chest. Clover hangs back with her arms crossed, then runs to him anyway. Let the moment breathe.
9. **Epilogue hook.** Something gathers the Gatekeeper's fading Shadow energy and slips away. Only one player sees a red tuft of fur.
