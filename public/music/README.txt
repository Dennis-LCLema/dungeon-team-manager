Background music. Drop MP3 files here with these names. Any slot without a file is silent.
The app picks the most specific file first, then falls back to the general one.

town.mp3          Safe zones and the Waystone      (town-0.mp3 ... town-8.mp3 for one layer only)
overworld.mp3     Wild areas and Rift entrances    (overworld-0.mp3 ... overworld-8.mp3)
labyrinth.mp3     Walking the Labyrinth floors     (labyrinth-1.mp3 ... labyrinth-8.mp3, by descent)
rift.mp3          Inside a Red Rift                (rift-1.mp3 ... rift-8.mp3; falls back to labyrinth.mp3)
dungeon.mp3       Dungeons you plan yourself       (falls back to labyrinth.mp3)
battle.mp3        Any battle
boss.mp3          Battles with a boss or Gatekeeper (falls back to battle.mp3)

Tips: loops of 1-3 minutes, about 2-4 MB each. Use CC0 or CC-BY music and credit artists in CREDITS.txt.
Each device plays its own music; the 🔊 button and slider in the top bar mute it or set the volume.
